Clearnet & darknet investigations: training and technical assistance

John Jay and NCFTA collaborate on investigative training and technical assistance covering online criminal activity. NW3C provides access to associated webinars and self-paced learning for justice professionals.

Consumer IoT database: expanded investigative resources

John Jay received equipment funding to develop a facility supporting digital forensics, threat intelligence, malware analysis, infrastructure protection, and online investigations. The project also addresses research capacity for emerging technologies.
